one ink · one paper · one accent

Warm paper,
drawn in ink.

A portable, framework-agnostic system. The entire palette is one warm near-black ink mixed into one warm cream at graded steps, with a single warm-orange accent used only for state.

The mechanism is the brand — copy the mechanism and you copy jojo.

foundations

Five signatures

01
Warm monochrome
One hue, mechanically. Every neutral is ink stepped into paper. Olive-warm, never blue-gray.
02
Sharp + pill
Near-square 4px cards; fully-rounded buttons, tags, and dots. No soft 12–16px cards.
03
The ink outline
A hard near-black 1px stroke for emphasis, beside a barely-there hairline for chrome.
04
Light display
36–72px at weight 400 with negative tracking. Hierarchy by size, not weight.
05
Flat, one accent
Depth is the card ladder, not shadow. The orange is link / active / focus only — never a fill.
